ABOUT CIRCA

Support that brings
school communities together.

CIRCA is a school-support and coordination platform designed to help authorized school teams organize responsibilities, communicate clearly and handle sensitive information carefully.

◎

Our purpose

Make school support workflows more organized by connecting school leadership, teachers, authorized support staff and parents through dedicated portals.

◇

How it is organized

Each school has its own workspace. The school owner manages staff invitations, departments, classrooms and role assignments. Staff must be approved before accessing protected features.

♙

Privacy by responsibility

Different roles should see different information. Health and discipline information should only be available to staff with appropriate authorization.
